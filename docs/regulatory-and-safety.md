# Regulatory and safety status

Colvera AI is currently a non-clinical research concept. Its eventual regulatory pathway cannot be declared until the intended use, claims, user, workflow, risk controls, and comparison with existing devices are defined.

## Present restrictions

- no patient-care use;
- no diagnosis or treatment recommendation;
- no unapproved access to patient data;
- no performance or generalization claims without reproducible evidence;
- no assumption that a future pathway will necessarily be 510(k), De Novo, or a particular device class.

Research involving institutional health data must follow the partner institution's IRB, privacy, security, contracting, and data-governance requirements.
